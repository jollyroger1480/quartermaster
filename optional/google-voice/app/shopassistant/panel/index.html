    Google Voice

    The assistant answers calls to a Google Voice number through its own browser window. Your normal browser and Google account stay untouched.

    1. Get a Google Voice number if you don't have one, at voice.google.com. To use it for your shop line, forward your shop number to it or give it out directly.
    2. Sign in. Press the button, sign in to Google in the window that opens, open Voice, then close that window.
    3. Check two Voice settings (gear icon, Calls): calls ring on Web, and Screen calls is off.
    4. Turn the assistant on from Home. The Voice window opens by itself and must stay open; you can minimise it.

    Good to know

    • If you answer on your own phone before the assistant's pickup delay runs out, it stays out of the call.
    • Calls are recorded on this computer. Some states require telling callers; add “Calls may be recorded” to your greeting if that applies to you.
    • Google doesn't offer an official way to automate Voice. This drives the web page, so a Google redesign can stop it answering until it is updated, and Google's terms restrict automated use.
